YT Pipeline

YT Pipeline is a personal tool used by its developer to prepare short videos and upload them, as private drafts, to the developer's own YouTube channels for review before publishing.

It is not a public service. It is used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account and YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads videos to the developer's own YouTube channels, as private videos.
  • Adds those videos to the developer's own playlists.
