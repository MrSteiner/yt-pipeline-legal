Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

YT Pipeline ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its developer. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from other people.

What the app accesses

With the developer's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to, and manage playlists on, the developer's own YouTube channels. It accesses only the Google account that the developer signs in with.

How information is stored

The sign-in credentials (tokens) that Google provides are stored only on the developer's own computer. They are used only to talk to YouTube on the developer's behalf.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
